## Overview
The site behaves like a hardcover book: navy case bands, cream pages, and an orange ribbon bookmark that runs down the right edge and grows as you scroll. Real product photography leads every section. The structure follows notedinstyle.co.uk: quick rail, use-case cards, client strip, "Exceptional as standard", customisation, gallery, case studies, quote.

## Colors
Navy is the dominant brand field; orange is the accent for the ribbon, primary buttons and small markers only. Cream is the page ground; white for cards and alternating bands. Night is for the top bar and footer.

## Typography
Montserrat for headings, buttons and labels, Open Sans for reading text. Uppercase only in the hero headline, hero sub-line and footer sign-off. Body never below 13px.

## Layout
Content max 1240px, bands full width. Sticky stacked use-case cards on desktop, plain stack on phones. Mosaic gallery pattern: normal, normal, tall, wide, normal, big, normal, wide, normal.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do use real YSIDRA photos and the real logo files in public/img.
- Do keep prices off the site; every product routes to a quote or WhatsApp.
- Don't add emojis, arrow glyphs, eyebrow labels or invented testimonials.
- Don't use Twitter/X; Instagram and LinkedIn only.
